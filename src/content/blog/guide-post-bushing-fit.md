body_en: |
  <p class="lead text-lg font-medium text-slate-800">
    The guiding system forms the alignment backbone of stamping dies and injection molds. If the clearance between the guide pillar (post) and bushing is too loose, upper and lower die components will experience misalignment, uneven punch cutting clearances, and accelerated mold wear. Conversely, if the clearance is too tight, frictional heating at high speeds can cause seizure (galling) and machine shutdown.
  </p>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">1. Sliding Guide Fits: H7 / g6 Standard</h2>
  <p>
    For standard stamping dies and plastic injection molds operating under moderate cycle speeds, the <strong>ISO H7/g6 fit</strong> is universally adopted as the primary precision sliding standard:
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>Bushing Bore Tolerance (H7):</strong> Internal diameter precision held within +0.015 / 0 mm (for Ø25 mm).</li>
    <li><strong>Guide Post OD Tolerance (g6):</strong> External diameter precision held within -0.007 / -0.020 mm (for Ø25 mm).</li>
    <li><strong>Initial Working Clearance:</strong> Typically <strong>0.007 mm to 0.035 mm</strong>, providing adequate room for an unbroken hydrodynamic lubrication film.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">3. Hardened Steel vs. Self-Lubricating Bronze Bushings</h2>
  <p>
    The pairing of materials between post and bushing is critical to preventing identical-metal galling:
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>SUJ2 / 20Cr Steel Guide Post + Hardened Alloy Bushing (HRC 60+):</strong> Delivers highest rigidity for precision stamping dies, but requires continuous forced oil or grease lubrication through spiral or diamond oil grooves.</li>
    <li><strong>SUJ2 Post + Sintered Graphite Bronze Bushing:</strong> Embedded solid graphite plugs provide micro-lubrication without requiring constant external oil. Ideal for cleanroom medical molds and automotive stamping dies where oil drops would contaminate parts.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">4. Ball Bearing Cage Guide Sets (Zero-Clearance Preload)</h2>
  <p>
    When stamping thin metal foils (&le; 0.2 mm) or high-speed connector terminals at 600+ strokes per minute, sliding clearances can cause punching burrs.
  </p>
  <div class="my-4 rounded border-l-4 border-borui-brass bg-borui-light p-4 text-sm text-slate-700">
    <strong>High-Speed Stamping Solution:</strong> Utilize <strong>Ball Bearing Cage Guide Sets (Ball Retainers)</strong>. High-precision grade-G10 steel balls operate under a controlled negative clearance (preload of -0.003 mm to -0.008 mm). Rolling friction replaces sliding friction, eliminating play and running virtually heat-free up to 1,200 strokes per minute.
  </div>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">一、精密滑动导向配合：H7 / g6 国际标准</h2>
  <p>
    在常规冲压连续模与注塑模具中，<strong>ISO H7/g6 基孔制间隙配合</strong>是最通用、最可靠的黄金搭配：
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>导套内孔公差（H7）：</strong> 以 Ø25 mm 为例，内孔公差带为 +0.021 / 0 mm。</li>
    <li><strong>导柱外径公差（g6）：</strong> 外径公差带为 -0.007 / -0.020 mm。</li>
    <li><strong>初始滑动间隙：</strong> 理论配合单边/双边间隙在 <strong>0.007 ~ 0.041 mm</strong> 之间，既能保证合模无晃动，又能为润滑油膜保留流动渗透空间。</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">三、硬化钢套 vs 自润滑石墨铜套应用选型</h2>
  <p>
    导柱与导套必须避免使用同质软材料摩擦，否则极易发生咬死黏结：
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>SUJ2（轴承钢）导柱 + 淬硬钢导套（HRC 60+）：</strong> 刚性最高、抗重击，适合重型冲裁及汽车覆盖件模具；必须配套螺旋或网状油槽定期加注润滑油。</li>
    <li><strong>SUJ2 导柱 + 镶石墨高力黄铜导套：</strong> 铜套基体镶嵌固体润滑石墨栓，摩擦发热时石墨自动在表面形成微米级润滑膜。适合无油洁净注塑（食品/医疗件）及不便人工频繁加油的复杂工位。</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">四、高速冲压滚珠导柱组（零间隙预压配合）</h2>
  <p>
    在冲切 0.2mm 以下超薄金属箔片或每分钟 600 次以上的高速接插件冲压中，传统滑动配合的微小间隙也会引起毛刺。
  </p>
  <div class="my-4 rounded border-l-4 border-borui-brass bg-borui-light p-4 text-sm text-slate-700">
    <strong>高速精密冲压方案：</strong> 采用<strong>高精度滚珠导向组件（Ball Cage Retainer）</strong>。精选 G10 级高精度钢珠，采用 <strong>-0.003 ~ -0.008 mm</strong> 的微量过盈（负间隙）预压装配，以纯滚动摩擦替代滑动摩擦，彻底消除侧向游隙，可在高达 1000~1200 SPM 冲速下无热胀咬死稳定运行。
  </div>
