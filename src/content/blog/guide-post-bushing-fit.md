body_en: |
  Mold guide posts and bushings rely on sliding fit without seizure over millions of cycles.

  **Common fit:** Post g6 (~0.01–0.03 mm clearance on Ø25) into bushing H7. Heavier molds or poor lubrication may need slightly more clearance — we follow your drawing callout.

  **Length and shoulder:** Post head height and bushing flange thickness must match plate stack. Mark shoulder lengths on section views, not only overall length.

  **Materials:** SUJ2 or 20Cr posts, nitrided for wear; bronze bushings for self-lube systems, hardened steel for press-fit into plates.

  **Inspection:** We check OD/ID and concentricity before shipment. If you specify fit class on the drawing, we hold it — otherwise confirm clearance on the RFQ.

  Custom lengths and oil groove patterns are standard at YZX Precision. Send plate stack section with post/bushing positions marked.
body_zh: |
  导柱与导套靠滑动配合长期工作，不能研死也不能间隙过大晃模。

  **常见配合：** 导柱 g6、导套 H7（例如 Ø25 约 0.01–0.03 mm 间隙）。大型模或润滑不良时可略加大间隙 — 以图纸标注为准。

  **长度与台阶：** 导柱头高、导套法兰厚须与面板叠厚一致。剖面图要标台阶长度，不要只标总长。

  **材质：** 导柱常用 SUJ2、20Cr，可氮化；自润滑系统用青铜导套，压入式用硬化钢导套。

  **检验：** 出货前检外径/内径及同轴度。图纸注明配合等级则严格按级；未注明请在询价中写清间隙要求。

  非标长度、油槽型式源智信均可加工。请提供模座剖面并标导柱导套位置。
