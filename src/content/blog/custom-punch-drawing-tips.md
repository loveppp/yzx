description_zh: 订购六角、台阶、斜度冲针时图纸应包含哪些尺寸 — 减少返工。
body_en: |
  Non-standard punches fail in quoting when the drawing omits one critical view. Include at minimum:

  1. **Plan and section views** — cross-section shape with all dimensions. For hex, mark across-flats, not corner-to-corner.
  2. **Material and hardness** — e.g. SKD11 HRC 58–62, or "same as sample."
  3. **Tolerance block** — default ±0.005 mm unless specific features need ±0.002 mm. Mark tight features individually.
  4. **Surface finish** — ground OD, EDM finish on shaped head, or polish Ra value.
  5. **Quantity and sample requirement** — 1pc sample before batch is common; state it on the RFQ.

  Acceptable file formats: STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF, PDF. A clear PDF with dimensions is enough to start. Physical samples can be measured and confirmed with a product drawing before production.

  YZX Precision returns quotes within 2 hours during business hours once the drawing is complete.
body_zh: |
  异形冲针询价拖慢，多半是图纸少了一个关键视图。至少应包含：

  1. **平面图与剖面图** — 截面形状及全部尺寸。六角标注对边距，不是对角线。
  2. **材质与硬度** — 如 SKD11 HRC 58–62，或注明「同来样」。
  3. **公差** — 默认 ±0.005 mm；特殊位置单独标 ±0.002 mm。
  4. **表面要求** — 外径磨削、异形头部放电面、或抛光 Ra 值。
  5. **数量与样品** — 批量前先做 1 件确认很常见，请在询价中说明。

  可接受 STEP、IGES、DWG、DXF、PDF。标注清楚的 PDF 即可起步；来样可测绘确认后再生产。

  源智信工作时间内收到完整图纸，通常 2 小时内回复报价。
