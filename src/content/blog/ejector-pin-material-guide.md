body_en: |
  <p class="lead text-lg font-medium text-slate-800">
    Ejector pins are the most heavily cycled mechanical components in plastic injection molds, undergoing continuous high-velocity reciprocating motion, frictional wear, and direct thermal shocks from molten resin. Selecting the incorrect grade of tool steel frequently leads to pin seizure, galling, surface flash on molded parts, or catastrophic fatigue failure.
  </p>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">1. SKD61 (JIS) / AISI H13 / DIN 1.2344 — The Universal Workhorse</h2>
  <p>
    SKD61 is a chromium-molybdenum hot-work tool steel and remains the global baseline choice for over 80% of standard injection tooling:
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>Dual Heat-Treatment Architecture:</strong> Through-hardened to HRC 38–42 in the core to preserve flexible toughness, then vacuum ion-nitrided on the surface to achieve a micro-hardness of HV 950–1100 (equivalent to HRC 68–72).</li>
    <li><strong>Thermal Shock Resistance:</strong> Excellent resistance to thermal cracking and softening at mold operating temperatures up to 550&deg;C.</li>
    <li><strong>Best Suited For:</strong> General-purpose engineering resins (PP, PE, ABS, PC, PA6) in standard cycle times.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">2. SKH51 (JIS) / AISI M2 / DIN 1.3343 — High-Speed Steel for Severe Duty</h2>
  <p>
    SKH51 contains significant tungsten, molybdenum, and vanadium alloys, offering dramatically enhanced compressive strength and hot hardness:
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>Uniform Through-Hardness:</strong> Heat-treated to a uniform HRC 60–64 across both surface and core without relying on a thin nitrided crust.</li>
    <li><strong>Anti-Deflection Rigidity:</strong> Possesses a high Young's modulus, preventing elastic deflection when ejecting deep-rib or high-drag optical moldings.</li>
    <li><strong>Best Suited For:</strong> Small diameters (&le; Ø2.5 mm), glass-fiber reinforced abrasive polymers (PBT+30%GF, PPS, LCP), and fast-cycling multi-cavity medical molds (e.g., syringe barrels).</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">4. Solutions for Thin Pin Buckling & Fracture</h2>
  <p>
    When ejector pin diameters fall below Ø2.0 mm with stroke lengths exceeding 50 mm, straight pins often buckle under high release pressure.
  </p>
  <div class="my-4 rounded border-l-4 border-borui-brass bg-borui-light p-4 text-sm text-slate-700">
    <strong>Design Recommendation:</strong> Transition from straight pins to <strong>Stepped Ejector Pins (Shoulder Pins)</strong> or <strong>Blade Ejector Pins</strong>. Stepping up the holding shank to Ø4.0 mm or Ø6.0 mm with a short active working tip length dramatically elevates the critical buckling load by over 400%.
  </div>

    顶针是注塑模具中往复运动频率最高、受摩擦磨损最剧烈的精密运动部件。顶针长期在数百摄氏度的塑料熔体冲击和频繁推出复位中工作，如果材质选择不当，轻则引起表面拉毛、拉伤模具镶件，重则导致卡死抱死、顶针折断而直接停产。

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">一、SKD61（JIS 热作模具钢 / AISI H13）— 标准注塑模默认基石</h2>
  <p>
    SKD61 兼具优异的抗高温回火软化能力与极佳的冲击韧性，占据了全球 80% 以上中大型塑料模具顶针的应用：
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>双重组织热处理工艺：</strong> 心部调质至 HRC 38–42 保持高韧性，外表面经离子或真空氮化处理，形成 HV 950–1100（折合 HRC 68–72）的高硬度渗氮耐磨层。</li>
    <li><strong>抗热疲劳特性：</strong> 在 550&deg;C 高温下长期作业不退火、不开裂。</li>
    <li><strong>推荐应用：</strong> 汽车内外饰件、家电外壳、PP / PE / ABS / PC / PA6 等常规工程塑料的注塑模具。</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">二、SKH51（JIS 高速钢 / AISI M2）— 严苛工况与细长顶针首选</h2>
  <p>
    SKH51 含有高比例的钨、钼、钒等强碳化物形成元素，具有极佳的高温红硬性与耐磨性：
  </p>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-3">
    <li><strong>内外通体高硬度：</strong> 整体淬火硬度均匀达到 HRC 60–64，不依赖表层薄氮化层，即使磨损后依然坚硬耐磨。</li>
    <li><strong>抗弯曲刚性好：</strong> 弹性模量高，深骨位、薄壁注塑件顶出时不易产生弹性弯曲变形。</li>
    <li><strong>推荐应用：</strong> 细针（&le; Ø2.5 mm）、加纤工程料（如 PA66+30%玻纤、PBT+GF、PPS、LCP）、高转速短周期医疗及电子连接器模具。</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="mt-8 text-2xl font-serif text-borui-navy">四、细长顶针防弯曲与断针设计锦囊</h2>
  <p>
    当顶针直径小于 Ø2.0 mm 且顶出行程超过 50 mm 时，普通直顶针极易失稳受压弯曲。
  </p>
  <div class="my-4 rounded border-l-4 border-borui-brass bg-borui-light p-4 text-sm text-slate-700">
    <strong>模具设计对策：</strong> 建议改用<strong>台阶托针（Shoulder Ejector Pin）</strong> 或 <strong>扁顶针（Blade Pin）</strong>。将固定部柄径加粗到 Ø4.0 或 Ø6.0 mm，前端仅保留最短有效出模行程，可将顶针的临界失稳压力提升 400% 以上，杜绝断针卡模。
  </div>
