body_en: |
  **SKD61 (JIS hot work tool steel)** is the default for most plastic mold ejector pins. It balances hardness (HRC 58–62 after heat treat) with toughness. Use SKD61 when the pin tip is not continuously exposed to melt temperatures above ~600°C and cycle times are normal.

  **SKH51 / SKH-9 (high-speed steel)** gives higher hardness (HRC 62–65) and better wear resistance on small diameters. Choose SKH51 when pins are thin (under Ø3 mm), high-cycle, or when you see early wear or breakage on SKD61.

  **Surface treatment:** Nitriding on the head and bearing length extends life without changing core properties. For release problems, polish to Ra 0.2–0.4 μm rather than changing material.

body_zh: |
  **SKD61（JIS 热作模具钢）** 是多数塑料模顶针的默认材质，热处理后 HRC 58–62，强韧兼顾。型腔温升不高、常规周期时优先选 SKD61。

  **SKH51 / SKH-9（高速钢）** 硬度更高（HRC 62–65），小直径更耐磨、更耐崩。直径小于 Ø3 mm、模次高、或 SKD61 早期磨损/断针时，可改 SKH51。

  **表面处理：** 针头部氮化可延长寿命；若粘模，可将表面抛光至 Ra 0.2–0.4 μm，不必先换材质。

  **图纸建议标注：** 材质、硬度范围、氮化层深（如需）、直径/长度公差。源智信按图加工，可发 CAD 或样品询价。
