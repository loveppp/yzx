Product family: Carbide Punches.
Product function: Wear-resistant punches with round or profiled working ends for repeat stamping. Carbide grade and edge geometry are selected together.
Configuration context: Type A straight round punch: Round working end with a straight body; confirm the Type A designation against the selected standard or drawing. Type T headed round punch: An enlarged retaining head provides axial retention; check the underside seat against the holder. Stepped and carbide-tipped designs: A larger shank supports the reduced tip; tipped designs require an agreed joint and load path. Square, rectangular and custom profiles: Define the cutting edge from the required hole; non-round tips also need rotational orientation. Hexagonal punch: For hexagonal holes; across-flats size, corners and tip length define the working geometry. Oval and obround punches: An ellipse differs from a straight-sided obround; define the exact outline in the drawing. Cut-off and precision piercing: Define cut-off edges and precision piercing by load and dimensional requirements; precision is not a fixed tolerance claim. Coating and wear options: Confirm coating compatibility with carbide grade, work material and edge condition; color does not establish performance.
